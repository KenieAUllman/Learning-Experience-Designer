How I work

I don’t start with the course. I start with the problem.

The right learning solution starts upstream. I look at the work people need to perform, what is getting in the way, and what evidence will tell us whether the intervention helped.

01

Diagnose

What should people be able to do, and what is preventing them from doing it now?

  • Needs analysis
  • Audience & workflow analysis
  • SME discovery
  • Performance gaps
02

Design

What combination of instruction, practice, and performance support will help people get there?

  • Curriculum architecture
  • eLearning & ILT/VILT
  • Scenario practice
  • Performance support
03

Measure

What evidence will tell us whether the learning worked, and what should change next?

  • Assessment
  • KPI development
  • Learning analytics
  • Program evaluation
