Who owns America’s pets?

Pet ownership spans generations, but the mix is changing. This reconstruction preserves the original Storyline interaction model while rebuilding it for the responsive web with keyboard controls, touch behavior, persistent state, and accessible alternatives.

Chart data uses a published 2024 generational share of U.S. pet owners reported by Forbes Advisor from APPA data. The four displayed generations total 95%, with the remaining share not represented in the source breakdown shown here. View source.

01 • Dogs

Dogs

Dogs remain the most widely owned pet category in the United States. Their popularity crosses household types because the category includes enormous variation in size, temperament, activity level, and the ways owners integrate dogs into daily life.

01
Companionship

Dogs are often deeply integrated into routines, recreation, travel, and family life.

02
Trainability

Many owners value the ability to build routines, communication, and shared activities through training.

03
Variety

The category spans a wide range of body sizes, energy levels, coat types, and temperaments.

Sources
  • APPA 2026 State of the Industry release
  • APPA 2025 Dog & Cat Report release
Hover, focus, or tap for a fact
Dog fact

APPA reported that 53% of U.S. households owned a dog in 2025, representing about 71 million households.

02 • Cats

Cats

Cats combine companionship with routines that can work well across many household types. Recent APPA reporting also shows cat ownership continuing to grow, particularly among younger owners.

01
Adaptable living

Cats can fit into a wide range of home sizes and indoor living arrangements.

02
Independent routines

Owners can build companionship around a pet that does not require the same outdoor routine as many dogs.

03
Companionship

Modern cat ownership increasingly emphasizes interaction, enrichment, training, and shared time.

Sources
  • APPA 2026 State of the Industry release
  • APPA 2025 Dog & Cat Report release
Hover, focus, or tap for a fact
Cat fact

APPA reported 53 million U.S. households owning a cat in 2025, up 5% year over year.

03 • Fish

Fish

Fish occupy a different corner of pet ownership: compact habitats, visual interest, enormous species variety, and care systems built around a controlled aquatic environment rather than a roaming companion animal.

01
Compact footprint

An aquarium creates a defined habitat rather than requiring free movement throughout a home.

02
Visual appeal

Aquariums combine animal care with movement, color, habitat design, and environmental observation.

03
Species variety

Freshwater and saltwater keeping can involve dramatically different species, habitats, and care requirements.

Sources
  • APPA Fish & Reptile Report overview
  • APPA 2025 Fish & Reptile Report release
Hover, focus, or tap for a fact
Fish fact

APPA’s 2024 Fish & Reptile report overview said 8% of U.S. households owned fish.
