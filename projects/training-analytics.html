Learning & Performance Analytics Case Study

Workforce Training Analytics

Building a measurement framework around 1,050 synthetic trainee records.

A reconstructed analytics project showing how I moved beyond course completion to examine placement patterns, retention, cohort maturity, downstream outcomes, and the limitations of what the data could actually support.

Role Learning / Performance Analyst
Tools Excel, pivot tables, cohort analysis, statistical analysis, survival analysis
Focus KPIs, program evaluation, retention, cohort maturity, decision support
Dataset 1,050 synthetic trainee records
Synthetic Data Disclosure

All employee-level information and metrics displayed in this reconstruction are synthetic. The analytical approach reflects methods I applied in professional learning-and-performance work, but no employer or employee data is shown here.

01 • Challenge

What happened after training?

Leadership needed a clearer way to understand what happened after training and how training conditions interacted with downstream workforce outcomes.

The information existed across records, but completion alone could not answer the questions that mattered. The analysis needed to connect training cohorts with placement timing, retention, promotion, tenure, and other operational variables while also acknowledging when the data was incomplete or too immature to support a confident conclusion.

05 • Reflection

Better questions were the real deliverable.

The biggest value of this project wasn’t a dashboard. It was creating a framework for asking better questions.

Learning data becomes useful when we stop asking only “Did they complete training?” and start asking how learning conditions interact with actual performance and workforce outcomes.

The analysis also reinforced the importance of restraint. A useful dashboard should surface patterns without pretending that every pattern is causal, every cohort is mature, or every metric means what its label seems to imply.
